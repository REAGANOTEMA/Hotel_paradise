# HOTEL PARADISE ON THE NILE — COMPLETE SQL COVERAGE MAP

Reagansoft Innovation Limited

This map defines the SQL/data ownership areas required by the hotel system. It does not claim that a table exists when the supplied project SQL does not define one; use it as the final coverage checklist for implementation.

## 01_RECEPTION — Reception
Required scope: front desk, check-in/out, guest registration, room assignment, folio actions, reception activity logs

## 02_RESERVATIONS — Reservations
Required scope: bookings, availability, confirmations, cancellations, deposits

## 03_ROOMS — Rooms
Required scope: room types, rooms, rates, room status, occupancy

## 04_GUESTS — Guests
Required scope: guest profiles, identification, stays, preferences, consent records

## 05_HOUSEKEEPING — Housekeeping
Required scope: room cleaning, assignments, inspections, linen/lost property

## 06_RESTAURANT — Restaurant
Required scope: tables, orders, menu items, dining bills

## 07_KITCHEN — Kitchen
Required scope: kitchen orders, preparation status, food production records

## 08_BAR — Bar
Required scope: bar menu, orders, stock usage, cashier/POS records

## 09_ROOM_SERVICE — Room Service
Required scope: in-room orders, delivery and posting to folios

## 10_POS — POS
Required scope: terminals, sales, shifts, receipts, voids/returns with audit trail

## 11_SWIMMING_POOL — Swimming Pool
Required scope: entries, services, charges, operational records

## 12_SPA — Spa
Required scope: appointments, treatments, charges, staff/service records

## 13_LAUNDRY — Laundry
Required scope: laundry jobs, items, charges, status

## 14_STORE_INVENTORY — Store & Inventory
Required scope: items, stock movements, locations, stock counts

## 15_PROCUREMENT — Procurement
Required scope: suppliers, purchase requests, purchase orders, receipts

## 16_MAINTENANCE — Maintenance
Required scope: work orders, assets, faults, completion records

## 17_FINANCE — Finance
Required scope: cash movements, expenses, approvals, financial controls

## 18_CASHIER — Cashier
Required scope: cashier sessions, collections, reconciliation

## 19_ACCOUNTING — Accounting
Required scope: accounts, journals, postings, financial reporting

## 20_AUDITOR — Auditor
Required scope: audit logs, review records, exception tracking

## 21_HR — HR
Required scope: staff, departments, attendance/HR records

## 22_EVENTS — Events & Conference
Required scope: event bookings, spaces, packages, charges

## 23_TRANSPORT — Transport
Required scope: vehicle/service bookings and charges

## 24_COMMUNICATION — Communication
Required scope: internal messages, notifications, calls where lawful/consented

## 25_USERS_SECURITY — Users & Security
Required scope: roles, permissions, password reset records, sessions, audit/security controls

## 26_REPORTING — Reporting
Required scope: operational and financial reporting support

## 27_EFRIS — URA EFRIS
Required scope: EFRIS invoice/receipt integration records and synchronization attempts

## 28_PAYMENTS — Payments
Required scope: payment transactions and provider integration records

## 29_SETTINGS — System Settings
Required scope: hotel configuration, tax/service-charge settings and system preferences

## 30_WEBSITE_INTEGRATION — Website Integration
Required scope: public website integration with the shared hotel database/API
